BRD – Dynamic Pricing

CompletedLast updated 16-11-2026 · Rate of interest worked out as base rate (by loan amount) + bureau rate (by score) + equity % of portfolio rate, calculated by the BRE after the CAMS fetch and again on fund selection, and kept the same across LOS, KFS and repayment.

1. Business requirement

The rate of interest (ROI) for a customer is worked out with this formula:

Final ROI = Base interest rate + Bureau incremental rate + Equity % of portfolio incremental rate

The system works out each part on its own, using the rules below, and shows the calculation to the customer in a simple, easy-to-understand way.

2. Parts of the ROI

2.1 Base interest rate

The base interest rate depends on the sanctioned loan amount.

Loan amountBase interest rate
Below ₹10 lakh10.50%
₹10 lakh to below ₹50 lakh10.30%
₹50 lakh to below ₹1 crore10.00%
₹1 crore to below ₹2 crore9.75%
₹2 crore to below ₹100 crore9.60%
₹100 crore and above9.50%

2.2 Bureau incremental rate

An extra rate is added based on the customer’s bureau score.

Bureau scoreIncremental rate
650 to 9000.00%
550 to 6490.30%
300 to 5490.40%
Below 3000.25%
NA (score not available)0.25%
NH (no history / new to credit)0.25%

Rules:

  • The system picks up the bureau score available for the customer.
  • The score is matched to its bucket in the table above.
  • For NA or NH customers, the incremental rate is 0.25%.
  • The customer is shown the bureau score (or the NA / NH status), the bucket and the incremental rate.

2.3 Equity % of portfolio incremental rate

The equity % of the portfolio is worked out from the collateral value of the pledged mutual funds:

Equity % of portfolio = (Collateral value of the equity mutual funds ÷ Collateral value of all the mutual funds) × 100

  • Top part: the total collateral value of the equity mutual funds.
  • Bottom part: the total collateral value of all the eligible pledged mutual funds, that is equity, hybrid and debt funds.

The percentage is then matched to this grid:

Equity % of portfolioIncremental rate
0% to below 70%0.00%
70% to below 85%0.10%
85% to 100%0.20%

5. When the BRE is called

The Business Rule Engine (BRE) is called two times in the customer journey to work out the eligibility and the ROI.

5.1 First call – after the CAMS fetch

  • When: after the mutual fund holdings are fetched from CAMS.
  • Input: the mutual fund data from CAMS and the other values needed for the calculation.
  • What the BRE does: processes the holdings from CAMS, works out the eligible loan amount using the LTV, works out the ROI from the inputs available, and returns the offer and eligibility details.
  • Result: the output fills the Curated Offers screen / dashboard. The customer continues through the CTA “Basis the CAMS Output” (or the CTA set up for this step).

5.2 Second call – on the Funds Selection page

  • When: on the Funds Selection page, after the customer selects the mutual funds for the loan.
  • Why: the funds the customer selects can be different from the holdings received from CAMS, so the eligibility and the ROI have to be worked out again for the final selection.
  • Input: the final list of selected funds and the other values needed.
  • What the BRE works out again: the eligible loan amount, the equity % of portfolio, the base interest rate (when the loan amount changes), the bureau incremental rate (as applicable) and the final ROI.
  • Result: the new ROI replaces the ROI from the first call wherever the inputs have changed.

6. Change in fund selection

  • Any change the customer makes on the Funds Selection page that changes the mix of pledged mutual funds calls the BRE again.

7. Where the ROI is used

The ROI is shown on these screens:

  • Curated Offers screen
  • Loan Application Summary screen
  • Withdrawal screen
  • Loan Details tab on the dashboard
  • Key Facts Statement (KFS)

The same ROI is used for:

  • The repayment schedule
  • The repayment worked out for each disbursement
  • Any other repayment figure shown to the customer that needs the ROI

The ROI shown on all these screens and the ROI used in the repayment calculations must always be the same.

8. System impact

BREROI worked out as base rate + bureau rate + equity % rate; called after the CAMS fetch, on the Funds Selection page, and again on any change in the selected funds.
LOSROI and its breakup shown on Curated Offers, Loan Application Summary, Withdrawal and the Loan Details tab; the ROI from the second call replaces the first one where the inputs changed.
LMSNew column “Type of mutual fund” (Equity / Hybrid / Debt) in the Collateral section of the Approved Securities master, and in its download template and upload.
Documents and repaymentThe same ROI in the KFS, the repayment schedule and each disbursement’s repayment.
