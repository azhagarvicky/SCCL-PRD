BRD – Loan Risk Stage Classification

CompletedLast updated 17-11-2026 · Every active loan is put into No Risk or Stage 1, 2 or 3 by comparing the loan outstanding with the collateral value at the L1, L2 and L3 LTV levels (revised grid of 22-09-2026), with a Stage column in LMS and daily alerts in Stage 2.

1. Requirement

The system puts every active loan into a risk stage. The stage depends on how the loan outstanding compares with the collateral value at each of the three LTV levels (L1, L2 and L3).

  • Hybrid funds are treated as equity for this classification.

2. LTV levels

As per the revised regulatory directions (revision of 22-09-2026), the LTV grid is as below. The equity values have changed; the debt values stay the same.

LTV levelEquity (incl. hybrid) – revisedEquity – earlierDebt
L145%65%75%
L248%68%78%
L349%73%83%

3. Collateral value at each level

For each active loan, the system works out the current collateral value at each LTV level:

A1Current collateral value at L1
A2Current collateral value at L2
A3Current collateral value at L3
  • Collateral value = Loan outstanding ÷ LTV of that level.
  • When a loan has more than one fund type, the equity or debt LTV is applied to each fund as per the approved collateral classification and LTV logic.

4. Risk stage

The loan outstanding is compared with A1, A2 and A3:

Loan outstandingRisk stage
Less than A1No Risk
A1 or more, but less than A2Stage 1
A2 or more, but less than A3Stage 2
A3 or moreStage 3

5. Stage column in LMS

  • A new column, Stage, is added on the Active Loans page and the Shortfall page.
  • The stage is worked out every time from the latest loan outstanding and the current collateral value (NAV), so it always shows the current position.

6. Shortfall and customer notification

  • Shortfall amount = Current outstanding – Collateral value at L1.
  • When a loan is in Stage 2, the customer gets an email and an SMS every day asking them to pledge more units or pay part of the principal.

7. System impact

LMSLTV grid L1 / L2 / L3 for equity (incl. hybrid) and debt; A1, A2 and A3 worked out for each active loan; new Stage column on the Active Loans and Shortfall pages; shortfall amount against L1.
CommunicationDaily email and SMS to the customer while the loan is in Stage 2.

8. Open points

  1. Formula: as written, Collateral value = Loan outstanding ÷ LTV, and then the loan outstanding is compared with that value. Should A1, A2 and A3 instead be the loan amount allowed at each level (Current collateral value × LTV of that level), so that the loan outstanding can be compared with them?
  2. Communication for Stage 1 and Stage 3: is anything sent to the customer, or to Ops, at these stages?
  3. Daily Stage 2 email and SMS: the content, the time of sending, and when it stops (for example, once the loan moves back to No Risk or Stage 1).
