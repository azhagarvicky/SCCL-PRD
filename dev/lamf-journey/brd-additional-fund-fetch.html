BRD – Additional Fund Fetch through Secondary Mobile Number

DraftLast updated 17-11-2026 · Fetch more mutual funds under the same PAN using a secondary mobile number or email ID, re-work the eligibility, carry the email into KYC and pledge through the mutually inclusive contact; one mobile number can hold two PAN profiles.

1. Overview

At onboarding, the customer’s mutual funds are fetched from MF Central using the mobile number and PAN given. A customer may hold more funds under the same PAN that are registered with another mobile number or an email ID. This BRD covers the additional fund fetch, where the customer gives a secondary mobile number or an email ID to fetch those funds too, and how it flows into KYC, pledging and the loan.

2. Process flow

3. Same mobile number with two PANs

  • One validated mobile number (M1) can be used with two different PANs (P1 and P2).
  • Each mobile number + PAN pair is a separate profile: M1P1 and M1P2.
  • Each profile goes through its own fund fetch, additional fund fetch, KYC, pledging and loan, and gets its own LAN (LAN 1 for M1P1, LAN 2 for M1P2).
  • The funds of one profile are never mixed with the funds of the other.

4. First fund fetch

  • The customer onboards with the mobile number (M1) and the PAN (P1).
  • The funds are fetched from MF Central for M1 + P1 and the eligibility is worked out. Example: Funds A, B and C.

5. Additional fund fetch

The customer can then ask for an additional fund fetch by giving another mobile number or an email ID. The funds are fetched only when the contact is linked with the same PAN. The outcomes are:

Contact givenLinked with the PAN?What happensExample (profile M1P1)
Same mobile number (M1)YesNothing new is fetched; the funds are already fetched.Funds A, B, C (no change)
Secondary mobile number (M2)YesThe funds linked with M2 + P1 are fetched and the eligibility is worked out again.Funds A, D, E
Email ID (E1)YesThe funds linked with E1 + P1 are fetched and the eligibility is worked out again. Funds already fetched are not added again.Funds A, B, C, D, E
Mobile number (M3) or email ID (E2)NoNo funds are available for this contact and PAN. The customer continues with the funds already fetched.No new funds
  • A fund that comes back in more than one fetch (for example, Fund A) is shown only once.
  • After every additional fund fetch, the eligibility is worked out again on the full list of funds.
  • For profile M1P2 the same rules apply to its own funds. Example: first fetch X, Y, Z; M2 gives X, U, V; E1 gives X, Y, Z, U, V.

6. Dedupe check

JourneyWhen the dedupe check runs
OrganicAfter onboarding, before the MF Central fund fetch.
LSPAfter the fund fetch and the additional fund fetch, before KYC.

7. KYC

  • If an email ID was used in the additional fund fetch, it is auto-filled in KYC and cannot be edited.
  • If more than one email ID was used in the additional fund fetch, the customer is shown radio buttons to choose one.

8. Pledging and loan

  • The funds are pledged giving priority to the contact that covers the most funds (the mutually inclusive contact). In the example, this is the email ID E1, as it covers Funds A, B, C, D and E.
  • The customer then completes the sanction letter, the e-Agreement and the mandate, and the LAN is submitted (LAN 1 for M1P1, LAN 2 for M1P2).

9. End to end flow

  1. The customer validates the mobile number (M1) and gives the PAN (P1 or P2).
  2. Organic journey: dedupe check.
  3. Funds are fetched from MF Central and the eligibility is worked out.
  4. The customer can do an additional fund fetch with a secondary mobile number or an email ID linked with the same PAN; the eligibility is worked out again.
  5. LSP journey: dedupe check.
  6. KYC, with the email ID from the additional fund fetch auto-filled (or chosen, if more than one).
  7. Pledging of the funds, giving priority to the mutually inclusive contact.
  8. Sanction letter, e-Agreement and mandate; the LAN is submitted.

10. System impact

LOSOption for an additional fund fetch with a secondary mobile number or email ID; combined fund list without duplicates; eligibility worked out again; email auto-filled and locked in KYC, or a radio button when there is more than one; two profiles on one mobile number, each with its own LAN.
