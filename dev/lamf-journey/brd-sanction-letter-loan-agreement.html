BRD – Sanction Letter & Loan Agreement Formats

1. Overview

Two documents are generated for the customer from fixed formats (templates), with the customer and loan details filled in by the system:

DocumentWhat it isFormat version
In-principle e-Sanction LetterThe sanction letter with the Key Fact Statement (KFS), the APR working and the illustrative repayment schedule.v1, 11-11-2026

This BRD lists what each document contains, the fields the system fills in, and the fixed values in the formats.

2. Sanction letter

2.1 Letter

  • Date, customer name, correspondence address (three lines) and customer ID.
  • Subject: request for financial assistance of the sanctioned amount.
  • Body: in-principle e-sanction of a loan against mutual funds for the stated purpose, as per the KFS, available once the documents are signed and the units are pledged / lien marked in favour of SCCL.
  • Support contact: lassupport@shriramcredit.in. Signed by Shriram Credit Company Limited.

2.5 Footnotes

  • The KFS is valid for 15 days from e-signing.
  • Interest for the gap days between disbursement and the first due is added to the first EMI.
  • Interest accrues daily on a reducing balance, on 365 days (366 in a leap year).
  • The processing fee is not refundable.
  • Lien is released within 30 days of full and final settlement.
  • The customer can exit within the cooling-off period by repaying the principal and the proportionate APR, without penalty; stamp duty, GST and statutory charges are not refunded.
  • The repayment bank account can be changed at most two times in the tenure.
  • An annual renewal charge applies when the loan is renewed or continued beyond the tenure.
  • Other standard terms: extra documents on request, SCCL’s discretion on disbursement, taxes extra, payments only from verified bank accounts, change of contact details within a month, indemnity, review and renewal at SCCL’s discretion.

Points in the clauses that tie to the LOS / LMS journey:

  • eNACH for 2 times the sanctioned amount or ₹1 crore, whichever is lower.
  • Further withdrawals within the loan limit after a repayment, on a fresh disbursal request.
  • If the customer does not withdraw within the cooling period and till the end of the tenure, these charges are levied: lien marking ₹450 + GST, stamp duty ₹200 + GST and lien removal ₹100 + GST. If not paid, SCCL recovers them from the pledged units (with invocation charges) and then releases the rest.
  • Excess payment is first adjusted against any dues, and the balance is refunded to the registered bank account, without interest.
  • A withdrawal repaid on the same day still attracts at least one day’s interest.

6. Open points

  1. The schedule of charges heading has a fixed agreement number (MF0000000000025). Should it be the customer’s LAN?
  2. Annexure numbering: the agreement refers to the schedule of charges and notice details as Annexure-1, but the schedule is headed Annexure 2, and the loan application form is also headed Annexure 2.
  3. Loan application form: the “Annual Income” row is filled with the monthly income field.
  4. KFS item 12 lists “exchange listed approved shares” as collateral; this product is against mutual funds only.
  5. KFS item 4 shows principal as 1 bullet at maturity, which fits the balloon / interest-only loan only. What should it show for an EMI loan?
  6. Stamp duty is shown with “+ GST”, and only for Tamil Nadu. Should it vary by the customer’s state?
  7. SCCL is called the party “of the Third Part” although the agreement has only two parties.
  8. The definition of “Event of Default” points to Clause 21; please check it against the final clause numbering.
  9. Wording fix: “pro data” should be “pro rata” (KFS item 9).
