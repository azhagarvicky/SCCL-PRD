BRD – Charges Waiver & Post-Pledge Drop-off – Scenario-wise

DraftLast updated 24-11-2026 · The Charges Waiver BRD written scenario by scenario (S 1 – S 7), point by point: approvals, communications, payment, de-pledge and UNO entries.

This is the scenario-wise version of the Charges Waiver BRD. The common rules are in section 2; section 3 lists, for each scenario, what happens point by point.

1. Purpose

  • When a customer pledges the mutual fund units and then does not complete the loan journey, charges are already incurred (lien marking, lien removal, stamp duty or processing fee). Today SCCL bears these charges.
  • OPS needs an option in LMS to reject or approve such a loan and, in the same request, waive these charges fully, partly or not at all.
  • The customer needs an option to pay the balance charges online, and OPS needs an option to de-pledge the units after the payment is confirmed.
  • Every waive-off has to be approved by three users: an OPS maker, an OPS checker and Treasury.

2. Common Rules (apply to every scenario)

2.1 Approvals Tab

  • LMS needs the Approvals tab for DRAFT loans also. Today it is available only for SUBMITTED, ACCOUNT OPENED and ACTIVE loans.
  • The Approvals tab needs five options: Waive-off charges, Add a note, Upload a file, Reject and Approve.
  • DRAFT loan: only Reject. SUBMITTED and ACCOUNT OPENED loans: Approve and Reject. ACTIVE loan: Waive-off from the ≡ menu only.
  • Until the loan is ACTIVE, the waive-off and the reject (or approve) go as one request.

2.2 Who Approves

Sanction amountMakerCheckerFinal approval
Any amountOPS L1OPS L2 or OPS L3Treasury L1, L2 or L3
  • OPS maker: note and file are mandatory.
  • OPS checker: can change the amounts; note is mandatory; file is mandatory only if the amounts are changed.
  • Treasury: can only approve or reject; note is mandatory.
  • LMS records every step in the Audit tab.

2.3 Waive-off Charges Table

  • OPS maker needs an option to select single or multiple charges using the checkbox, and to enter the amount to be waived off for each selected charge. LMS should not allow an amount more than the charge.
  • The waive-off type comes from the amount left to pay: No, Partial or Full waive-off.
  • Columns: Charges Name, Amount of Charges, Waived off Charges, Amount to be waived off, Payable Charges, with the total.

2.4 Charges Payment and De-pledge

  • The customer pays through the payment link: LOS → OTP → Pay Charges page (only the charges and the amount payable are shown). Only the full amount in one payment; partial payment is not allowed.
  • Payment failed: nothing changes and the customer can pay again.
  • Receipt number format: RCPT/LAMF/{{Payment Year}}/{{Payment Month}}/{{Number of receipts as 4 digit}}.
  • The waived amount is never posted in UNO.
  • If the customer does not pay, the system sends a reminder SMS and mail once a month. The units stay pledged until then. [Template – to be added]

3. Scenarios

3.1 S 1 – DRAFT: customer dropped off before pledging the mutual funds

  • Charges: None.
  • What OPS can do: No action.
  • LAN status can change to: INACTIVE (after 30 days)
  1. The customer starts the journey and the LAN is created in DRAFT, but the mutual fund units are not pledged.
  2. After 30 days from the LAN creation date, the LAN status changes to INACTIVE automatically.
  3. No charges, no waive-off, no de-pledge and no accounting entry.

3.2 S 2 – DRAFT: customer dropped off after pledging the mutual funds

  • Charges: Lien Marking ₹450 + GST (₹531) and Lien Removal ₹100 + GST (₹118).
  • What OPS can do: Reject, with no, full or partial waive-off.
  • LAN status can change to: REJECTED
  • Day 30 from the LAN creation date, if the LAN is not submitted: the system sends the customer an SMS and mail – “Your application is not complete and your Mutual Fund units are pledged. Please complete the journey.” [Template – to be added]
  • The application is not rejected because of this. OPS maker rejects a pledged DRAFT loan only when the customer asks to remove the lien.
  • Day 31: LMS sends OPS a mail that 30 days are completed, the journey is not completed and the lien is marked, so OPS checks with the customer to complete the journey or to send a mail to de-pledge. Until then, the lien stays marked with SCCL. [Template – to be added]

S 2.1 – Reject Without Waive-off

  1. OPS maker selects the charges, enters the amount to be waived off, adds the note, uploads the file and clicks Reject. Nothing is waived and the LAN status does not change yet.
  2. LMS sends a mail to the OPS checker to log in and approve or reject the request. [Template – to be added]
  3. OPS checker verifies the request, adds the note (and uploads a file if the amounts are changed) and approves. LMS sends a mail to Treasury. [Template – to be added]
  4. Treasury adds the note and approves. The waive-off is applied and the LAN status changes to REJECTED. LMS sends a mail to OPS and Treasury. [Template – to be added]
  5. The system sends the customer an SMS and mail with the payment link for ₹649.00. [Template – to be added]
  6. The customer opens the link, verifies the mobile number with an OTP in the LOS and pays the full amount in one payment.
  7. On success, the paid amount is posted in UNO (entry below), the receipt (RCPT/LAMF/2026/11/0149) is saved in the LMS Documents tab, and the system sends a mail to Treasury and OPS. [Template – to be added]
  8. Treasury confirms the payment in LMS. LMS sends a mail to OPS to initiate the de-pledge. [Template – to be added]
  9. The system sends the customer an SMS and mail with the payment receipt attached. [Template – to be added]
  10. OPS maker checks that no charges are pending and initiates the de-pledge. OPS checker approves the de-pledge.

3.7 S 7 – ACTIVE: customer dropped off after the Loan Lodgement (loan account active)

  • Charges: Processing Fee*, Bounce, Penal, Loan Renewal, AMC and Bank Swap charges.
  • What OPS can do: Waive-off only, from the ≡ menu → Waive-off page. The loan can't be rejected at this stage.
  • LAN status can change to: No change – stays ACTIVE

S 7.1 – Without Waive-off

  1. The charges stay in the Statement of Account and are collected before the loan is closed.
  2. No approval, no communication.

S 7.2 – With Full Waive-off

  1. OPS maker opens ≡ → Waive-off, selects the charges, enters the full amount and clicks Waive-off. The request goes to the Approvals tab.
  2. LMS sends a mail to the OPS checker. [Template – to be added]
  3. OPS checker verifies and approves. LMS sends a mail to Treasury. [Template – to be added]
  4. Treasury approves. The waived amount is posted in the Statement of Account with a waiver narration. LMS sends a mail to OPS and Treasury. [Template – to be added]
  5. The LAN stays ACTIVE. The waived amount is never posted in UNO.

S 7.3 – With Partial Waive-off

  1. Same steps as S 7.2, with part of the charge waived.
  2. The Amount column shows the balance, which is collected before the loan is closed. The LAN stays ACTIVE.

If the OPS checker or Treasury rejects the request, the request is cancelled: nothing is waived, the LAN status does not change, and the maker gets a mail and can raise a new request. [Template – to be added]
