BRD – Charges Waiver & Post-Pledge Drop-off – Scenario-wise

In ReviewLast updated 28-11-2026 · Waive charges (none, partial or full) with reject or approve, scenario by scenario (S 1 – S 7): approvals, mails, payment, de-pledge and UNO entries.

The Charges Waiver BRD, written scenario by scenario for an easy read from start to end. Section 2 has the rules that apply to every scenario; section 3 walks through each scenario step by step, with the sample mails and the UNO entry; sections 4 to 6 have the communications, the UNO account codes and the open points.

1. Purpose

  • When a customer pledges the mutual fund units and then drops off the journey, charges are already incurred (lien marking, lien removal, stamp duty or processing fee). Today SCCL bears these charges, and the customer has no option to pay them.
  • OPS needs an option in LMS to reject or approve such a loan and, in the same request, waive these charges fully, partly or not at all.
  • The customer needs an option to pay the balance charges online, and OPS needs an option to de-pledge the units after the payment is confirmed.
  • For an ACTIVE loan, OPS needs an option to waive charges, and the Statement of Account (SOA) is updated.

2. Rules for Every Scenario

2.1 Who Approves

MakerCheckerFinal approval
OPS L1OPS L2 or OPS L3Treasury L1, L2 or L3
  • This applies to any sanctioned amount. An approval without a waive-off (S 5.1, S 6.1) needs only the OPS maker and the OPS checker.
  • The note is mandatory for all three. The file is mandatory for the maker, and for the checker only if the checker changes the waive-off. Treasury can only approve or decline. Every step is saved in the Audit tab.
  • If the maker submits without the note or the file, LMS shows “Please add the note and upload a file.”
  • Once the maker raises a rejection request, the customer cannot continue that loan application in the LOS, and after the LAN is REJECTED the customer cannot move to any next step of it (LOS shows a message). So no charge can be added after the request. The customer can still start a fresh loan application. If the checker or Treasury declines the request, the customer can continue the existing application. (LOS change – PRD Pending Changes, CR-19.)
  • A DRAFT loan cannot be approved. For a rejection request (DRAFT, SUBMITTED or ACCOUNT OPENED), the checker and Treasury see the CTAs Accept Rejection and Decline Rejection – they accept or decline the rejection request, not the loan. Approve is used only to approve a SUBMITTED or ACCOUNT OPENED loan (S 5.1 – S 5.3, S 6.1 – S 6.3).

2.2 Charges and Waive-off

  • S 2 has Lien Marking ₹531 and Lien Removal ₹118. From S 3, LMS shows the customer's Processing Fee* (₹1,768.82) in four rows – Processing Fee ₹883.82, Lien Marking ₹531, Lien Removal ₹118 and Stamp Duty ₹236. LMS shows every charge with GST included, not as a separate GST amount.
  • Lien Marking, Lien Removal and Stamp Duty can only be waived in full. The Processing Fee and the other charges can be waived by any amount.
  • The waived amount includes GST. Penal charges carry no GST.
  • Each request is a No waive-off, Partial waive-off or Full waive-off, set by LMS from the amount left to pay.
  • Until the LAN is ACTIVE, the waive-off goes with the Reject or Approve in the Approvals tab as one request. For an ACTIVE loan, it is a separate request from the ≡ menu → Waive-off page, approved in the Approvals tab.

2.3 Customer Payment, Receipt and UNO

  • The link in the rejection SMS and mail opens the LOS. The customer verifies the mobile number with an OTP, and the only option the LOS then shows is to pay the charges – the full payable amount, including GST, in one payment. The de-pledge is initiated only after the customer pays. A failed payment changes nothing, and the customer can pay again. If the customer does not pay, a reminder SMS and mail is sent every month. (sample mail · SMS row 39) To be confirmed
  • For a REJECTED LAN with a balance payable, OPS maker (OPS L1) can click Resend Payment Link in LMS whenever the customer asks for the link. After the confirmation pop-up, the customer gets the same SMS and mail as the monthly reminder, with the same payment link. No checker or Treasury approval is needed, and LMS records it in the Audit tab. The CTA is disabled while a payment is pending Treasury confirmation, after the payment is approved, and for a full waive-off. (sample mail · SMS row 39) To be confirmed
  • On a successful payment, the amount is posted in UNO (each charge separately, one IGST line, Subaccd = LAN), and a receipt RCPT/LAMF/YYYY/MM/NNNN is saved in the LMS Documents tab. A duplicate or excess payment is refunded through the existing refund option.
  • The SOA is updated only when the LAN status is ACTIVE. For a DRAFT, SUBMITTED or ACCOUNT OPENED loan, a payment updates UNO only, not the SOA.
  • Whenever LMS updates the SOA of an ACTIVE LAN (a payment or a waive-off), it updates the database at the same time, so the SOA and the database always match, with no gap.
  • After every payment (the UNO entry is posted first, automatically), Treasury cross-checks that it is received and approves or rejects it. When Treasury approves, the balance payable is ₹0, so LMS enables the Initiate De-pledge CTA (straight away after a full waive-off). OPS maker initiates the de-pledge and OPS checker approves it.
  • If Treasury rejects the payment, the balance and the de-pledge do not change. OPS gets an internal mail that the payment is not received as per the entries, and the customer is told that the payment is not received and that any debited amount will be returned by the payment gateway. If the payment was actually received, Treasury handles it in the backend.

Each payable case links its own LOS payment journey and receipt (the amounts differ). S 4, S 5 and S 6 have the same amounts as S 3, so they use the S 3 screens.

3. Scenarios

3.1 S 1 – DRAFT: customer dropped off before pledging the mutual funds

Charges None.
What OPS can do No action.
LAN status can change to INACTIVE (on day 31)

Customer starts the journey→LAN created – DRAFT→Units not pledged→Day 31 – INACTIVE
  1. The LAN is created in DRAFT, but the mutual fund units are not pledged.
  2. On day 31 from the LAN creation date, the LAN status changes to INACTIVE automatically.
  3. No charges, no waive-off, no de-pledge and no accounting entry.

3.2 S 2 – DRAFT: customer dropped off after pledging the mutual funds

Charges Lien Marking ₹531 and Lien Removal ₹118 (full waive-off only).
What OPS can do Reject, with no, full or partial waive-off.
LAN status can change to REJECTED

Customer starts the journey→LAN created – DRAFT→Units pledged→Customer asks to remove the lien→Rejection request→Checker→Treasury→Rejected & Without Waive-off→Customer Payment→Receipt & UNO Entry→Treasury Confirmation→De-pledge

Before the rejection (pledged DRAFT, journey not completed)

  1. On day 30 from the LAN creation date, the customer gets an SMS and mail that the application is not complete and the units are pledged, so please complete the journey. The application is not rejected because of this. (sample mail · SMS row 40) To be confirmed
  2. On day 31, OPS gets a mail to check with the customer to complete the journey, or to ask the customer to send a mail to de-pledge the units. Until then, the lien stays marked with SCCL. LMS event mail triggered to OPS, Cc Treasury. (alert 21) To be confirmed
  3. OPS maker rejects a pledged DRAFT loan only when the customer asks by mail to remove the lien.

S 2.1 – Reject Without Waive-off

  1. OPS maker (OPS L1) selects the charges, enters the amounts, adds the note (mandatory), uploads the file (mandatory) and clicks Reject. Nothing is waived and the LAN status does not change yet. LMS event mail triggered to Treasury, Cc OPS, to approve or reject quickly. (alert 12) To be confirmed
  3. Treasury (Treasury L1, L2 or L3) adds the note (mandatory) and approves the rejection request (Accept Rejection). The waive-off is applied and the LAN status changes to REJECTED. LMS event mail triggered to OPS, Cc Treasury. (alert 13) To be confirmed
  4. The customer gets an SMS and mail with the payment link for ₹649.00. (sample mail · SMS row 36) To be confirmed
  5. The customer opens the link, verifies the mobile number with an OTP in the LOS and pays the full amount in one payment. (LOS payment journey · receipt)
  6. The paid amount is posted in UNO (each charge separately, entry below). The SOA is not updated, as the LAN is not ACTIVE. The receipt is saved in the LMS Documents tab. LMS event mail triggered to Treasury, Cc OPS, to confirm the payment is received. (alert 14) To be confirmed
  7. Treasury cross-checks that the payment is received and approves it (or rejects it if it is not received). The balance payable is now ₹0, so LMS enables the Initiate De-pledge CTA. LMS event mail triggered to OPS, Cc Treasury. The customer gets an SMS and mail with the receipt. (alert 15 · sample mail · SMS row 38) To be confirmed
  8. OPS maker initiates the de-pledge and OPS checker approves it.

3.5 S 5 – SUBMITTED: customer dropped off after the E-Mandate

Charges Processing Fee ₹883.82 (any amount), Lien Marking ₹531, Lien Removal ₹118 and Stamp Duty ₹236 (full only) – together the Processing Fee* ₹1,768.82 the customer sees.
What OPS can do Approve or reject, with no, full or partial waive-off.
LAN status can change to ACCOUNT OPENED or REJECTED

Customer starts the journey→LAN created – DRAFT→Units pledged→Sanction Letter→Loan Agreement e-Sign→E-Mandate→SUBMITTED→Approve or Reject request→Checker→Treasury (if waived)→ACCOUNT OPENED / REJECTED

At this stage OPS does the Loan Account Opening in LMS with Approve or Reject. Approve moves the LAN from SUBMITTED to ACCOUNT OPENED; Reject moves it straight to REJECTED. With this BRD, OPS chooses the waive-off with both: Approve with no, full or partial waive-off (S 5.1 – S 5.3), or Reject with no, full or partial waive-off (S 5.4 – S 5.6).

  1. OPS maker (OPS L1) adds the note (mandatory), uploads the file (mandatory) and clicks Approve without a waive-off. LMS event mail triggered to OPS Checker, Cc Treasury, to approve or reject quickly. (alert 16) To be confirmed

  3. The customer gets no SMS or mail at this stage.
  4. LMS shows the charges split, each with GST – Processing Fee ₹883.82, Lien Marking ₹531, Lien Removal ₹118 and Stamp Duty ₹236 (₹1,768.82 in all; the customer sees one Processing Fee*). They are posted in UNO, each charge separately, at the first withdrawal or repayment.

3.6 S 6 – ACCOUNT OPENED: customer dropped off after the Loan Account Opening

Charges Processing Fee ₹883.82 (any amount), Lien Marking ₹531, Lien Removal ₹118 and Stamp Duty ₹236 (full only) – together the Processing Fee* ₹1,768.82 the customer sees.
What OPS can do Approve or reject, with no, full or partial waive-off.
LAN status can change to ACTIVE or REJECTED

Customer starts the journey→LAN created – DRAFT→Units pledged→SUBMITTED→Loan Account Opening→ACCOUNT OPENED→Approve or Reject request→Checker→Treasury (if waived)→ACTIVE / REJECTED

At this stage OPS does the Loan Lodgement in LMS with Approve or Reject. Approve moves the LAN from ACCOUNT OPENED to ACTIVE; Reject moves it straight to REJECTED. With this BRD, OPS chooses the waive-off with both: Approve with no, full or partial waive-off (S 6.1 – S 6.3), or Reject with no, full or partial waive-off (S 6.4 – S 6.6).

S 6.1 – Approve Without Waive-off

  1. OPS maker (OPS L1) adds the note (mandatory), uploads the file (mandatory) and clicks Approve without a waive-off. LMS event mail triggered to OPS Checker, Cc Treasury, to approve or reject quickly. (alert 16) To be confirmed

3.7 S 7 – ACTIVE: customer dropped off after the Loan Lodgement

Charges Processing Fee, Lien Marking, Lien Removal, Stamp Duty, Bounce, Penal, Loan Renewal, AMC and Bank Swap.
What OPS can do Waive-off only, from the ≡ menu → Waive-off page. The loan cannot be rejected.
LAN status can change to No change – stays ACTIVE

LAN ACTIVE→≡ → Waive-off page→Waive-off request→Approvals tab – Checker→Treasury→SOA updated

S 7.1 – Without Waive-off

  1. Nothing is selected on the Waive-off page, so the Waive-off button stays disabled and no request is raised.
  2. The charges stay in the SOA and are collected as usual.

S 7.2 – With Full Waive-off

  1. OPS maker (OPS L1) opens ≡ → Waive-off, selects the charges, adds the note (mandatory), uploads the file (mandatory) and clicks Waive-off. On “Yes, Waive Off”, the request goes to the Approvals tab. LMS event mail triggered to OPS Checker, Cc Treasury, to approve or reject quickly. (alert 18) To be confirmed
  2. OPS checker (OPS L2 or OPS L3) approves the request in the Approvals tab with a note (mandatory). LMS event mail triggered to Treasury, Cc OPS, to approve or reject quickly. (alert 19) To be confirmed
  3. Treasury (Treasury L1, L2 or L3) gives the final approval with a note (mandatory). LMS event mail triggered to OPS, Cc Treasury. (alert 20) To be confirmed
  4. LMS updates the SOA (the waive-off is a credit with a waiver narration) and the database at the same time (section 2.3). Nothing is posted in UNO. The LAN stays ACTIVE.
  5. The customer gets no communication.

S 7.3 – With Partial Waive-off

  1. Same as S 7.2, with part of a charge waived (Processing Fee and the other charges only).
  2. Example: ₹500 waived (₹423.73 charge plus ₹76.27 IGST) – the SOA balance goes from ₹1,768.82 to ₹1,268.82.
  3. The balance is collected as usual.
