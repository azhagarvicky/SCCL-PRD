BRD – DRAFT LAN Rejection

DraftLast updated 16-11-2026 · Reject a loan application in DRAFT status in LMS when the mutual fund units are pledged (DRAFT → REJECTED, then de-pledge); unpledged DRAFT LANs move to INACTIVE after 30 days.

1. Background

Today the OPS team can reject a loan application in LMS only after it is SUBMITTED. A loan application in DRAFT status cannot be rejected. When the customer drops off after the mutual fund units are lien-marked (pledged) but before the application is submitted, the LAN stays in DRAFT and the units stay lien-marked: the lien can be removed (de-pledged) only for a REJECTED or a CLOSED LAN, and a DRAFT LAN can be neither.

2. Objective

Allow the OPS team to reject a DRAFT loan application in LMS when the customer has pledged the mutual fund units, so that the LAN status becomes REJECTED and the units can be de-pledged.

3. Scope

  • In scope: DRAFT LANs where the lien marking (pledge) is successful.
  • Out of scope: DRAFT LANs that are not pledged – they move from DRAFT to INACTIVE automatically (rule below); SUBMITTED and later statuses – the existing rejection applies; the charges on rejection – covered by the BRD “Charges Waiver & Post-Pledge Drop-off”.

4. LAN status rules

  • REJECTED – the application is rejected by OPS (new: also from DRAFT).
  • CLOSED – only an ACTIVE loan account is closed (foreclosure, tenure completion or invocation): ACTIVE → CLOSED. A LAN is never closed from DRAFT.
  • De-pledge (lien removal) is allowed only for a REJECTED or a CLOSED LAN. So for a pledged DRAFT LAN, rejection is the only way to remove the lien.
  • INACTIVE – a DRAFT LAN that is not pledged within 30 days from the LAN generated date (automatic).

6. Business rules

  • LMS must give an Approvals section in the LAN details for a DRAFT LAN (today it is not available for DRAFT loans), with a Reject action.
  • The Reject action is available only for a DRAFT LAN whose units are pledged. For a DRAFT LAN that is not pledged, Reject is not offered.
  • A pledged DRAFT LAN is not expired automatically. OPS rejects it manually:
    • after 30 days from the LAN generated date, when the customer has not submitted the application; or
    • at any time before that, when the customer asks for the lien removal.
  • Who can reject depends on the loan amount: below ₹5 lakh – OPS L1 only; ₹5 lakh and above – OPS L2 or L3 only.
  • A rejection reason must be captured – free text for now (a fixed list of reasons later) – and the rejection (reason, user, date and time) is recorded in the Audit.
  • Rejection needs one confirmation from the OPS user (the existing pop-up confirmation); once confirmed it is final and cannot be revoked.
  • On confirmation the LAN status changes from DRAFT to REJECTED and the LAN is listed with the rejected applications; the units can then be de-pledged.
  • A DRAFT LAN that is not pledged within 30 days from the LAN generated date moves to INACTIVE automatically – no OPS action.
  • The charges applicable on the rejection (lien marking, lien removal, and processing fee / stamp duty where incurred), their waive-off, the payment link and the de-pledging follow the BRD “Charges Waiver & Post-Pledge Drop-off” (scenarios 2 – 4).
  • Customer communication on rejection: the customer is told that the loan application has been rejected and the units are still lien-marked (with the payable charges and payment link where applicable, as per the Waiver BRD).
  • LOS: when the customer logs in after the rejection, the Curated Offers page shows a fresh product card (start a new application) instead of continuing the rejected one.

7. Flow

  1. The customer generates the LAN in LOS → LAN status DRAFT.
  2. If the units are not pledged within 30 days from the LAN generated date → the system moves the LAN from DRAFT to INACTIVE.
  3. If the units are pledged and the customer drops off before submitting → the LAN stays DRAFT (no automatic expiry).
  4. OPS (L1 below ₹5 lakh; L2 / L3 for ₹5 lakh and above) opens the DRAFT LAN in LMS → Approvals → Reject – after 30 days, or earlier on the customer’s request for lien removal.
  5. OPS enters the rejection reason and confirms (final).
  6. LAN status DRAFT → REJECTED; the rejection is recorded in the Audit.
  7. Customer is informed: application rejected, units still lien-marked (charges and payment link as per the Waiver BRD).
  8. De-pledge of the units: at once when the charges are fully waived; otherwise after the customer pays and the Treasury team confirms the payment in LMS (Waiver BRD).
  9. Next login in LOS → the Curated Offers page shows a fresh product card.
