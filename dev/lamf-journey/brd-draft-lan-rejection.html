BRD – DRAFT LAN Rejection

DraftLast updated 16-11-2026 · Reject a loan application in DRAFT status in LMS when the mutual fund units are pledged; unpledged DRAFT LANs move to INACTIVE after 30 days.

1. Background

Today the OPS team can reject a loan application in LMS only after it is SUBMITTED. A loan application in DRAFT status cannot be rejected. When the customer drops off after the mutual fund units are lien-marked (pledged) but before the application is submitted, the units stay lien-marked and SCCL has no way to close the application.

2. Objective

Allow the OPS team to reject a DRAFT loan application in LMS when the customer has pledged the mutual fund units, so that the application can be closed and the units de-pledged.

3. Scope

  • In scope: DRAFT LANs where the lien marking (pledge) is successful.
  • Out of scope: DRAFT LANs that are not pledged – they move from DRAFT to INACTIVE automatically (rule below); SUBMITTED and later statuses – the existing rejection applies; the charges on rejection – covered by the BRD “Waiver of Charges and Processing Fee”.

5. Business rules

  • LMS must give an Approvals section in the LAN details for a DRAFT LAN (today it is not available for DRAFT loans), with a Reject action.
  • The Reject action is available only for a DRAFT LAN whose units are pledged. For a DRAFT LAN that is not pledged, Reject is not offered.
  • Rejection needs one confirmation from the OPS user (the existing confirmation); once confirmed it is final and cannot be undone.
  • On confirmation the LAN status changes from DRAFT to REJECTED and the LAN is listed with the rejected applications.
  • A DRAFT LAN that is not pledged within 30 days from the LAN generated date moves to INACTIVE automatically – no OPS action.
  • The charges applicable on the rejection (lien marking, lien removal, and processing fee / stamp duty where incurred), their waive-off, the payment link and the de-pledging follow the BRD “Waiver of Charges and Processing Fee” (scenarios 2 – 4).

6. Flow

  1. The customer generates the LAN in LOS → LAN status DRAFT.
  2. If the units are not pledged within 30 days from the LAN generated date → the system moves the LAN from DRAFT to INACTIVE.
  3. If the units are pledged and the customer drops off before submitting → the LAN stays DRAFT.
  4. OPS opens the DRAFT LAN in LMS → Approvals → Reject.
  5. OPS confirms the rejection (final).
  6. LAN status DRAFT → REJECTED.
  7. Charges, customer communication and de-pledging as per the Waiver BRD.
